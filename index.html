PING A DANGER

Project Description:

Danger Alert System is a robust Python and React-based safety solution designed for large industrial sites. It enables employees to instantly alert the safety department about threats, dangers, or emergency situations, ensuring a swift response. This modern alternative to traditional alert systems enhances workplace safety with real-time notifications, intuitive user experience, and reliable communication.
