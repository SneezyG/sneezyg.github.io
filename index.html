Project Description:

Guardian - Emergency Alert System is a robust Python and React based safety platform for large industrial sites, enabling instant employee alerts and real-time responses to emergencies. Designed to replace outdated systems, it combines intuitive UX, multi-channel notifications, zone-based location tracking and embedded safety tips — promoting not just reaction, but a culture of prevention and preparedness.
